Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Humbul Slides is an internal publishing tool. It is used only by the owner of the Humbul accounts to send slideshow posts to their own TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not have other users.

Data we access

With the account owner's permission, the tool uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to read basic profile information (open ID, display name) and to upload slideshow drafts to the owner's TikTok inbox. It does not read videos, comments, messages, followers or any other user data.

Storage

Access and refresh tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and are used only to upload drafts. Slideshow images published on this site are public marketing content created for Humbul.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services). Contact: dogukantemizyurek@gmail.com
